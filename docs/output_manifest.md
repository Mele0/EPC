# Output manifest

Everything under `EPC_OUTPUT_DIR` is generated by `code/scripts/reproduce_all.py`. This
directory defaults to `outputs/` locally and is set to `/results` by the Code Ocean
driver. This note maps the quantities reported in the manuscript to the files that
produce them, so any number can be traced back to the code that computed it.

Result tables are written to `$EPC_OUTPUT_DIR/tables/`, the main-text tables to
`$EPC_OUTPUT_DIR/tables/paper/`, and figures to `$EPC_OUTPUT_DIR/figures/` as PDF and
PNG. Markdown reports are also written below `$EPC_OUTPUT_DIR/tables/`; no generated
artifact is written into the read-only code or input trees.

## England and Wales analysis

Driven by `analysis.run_analysis`, which builds the within-UPRN cohort once and writes:

- `register_year_trend.csv` - annual mean asset rating, building and standard
  emission rates and median primary energy; the basis for the register trend and the
  metered-energy comparison.
- `cohort_funnel.json` - the certificate-to-cohort funnel behind Figure 2c and
  Supplementary Table S2.
- `within_building_straddle_by_fuel.csv`, `no_measure_change_straddlers.csv` - the
  within-UPRN change by fuel and period, and the no-recorded-works calibration sample.
- `multiplier_components.csv`, `emissions_ratio_validation.json` - the fuel-specific
  BER, SER and asset-rating ratios of Table 1, and the predicted against observed
  electricity re-pricing ratio.
- `event_study_electric_gas.csv` - the building-fixed-effect event-study coefficients
  (Supplementary Table S4).
- `section_percentage.json` - the accounting against building decomposition of the
  measured straddler improvement.
- `fixed_factor_recompute.json` - the transparent fixed-accounting bridge: per-fuel
  multipliers, the re-rated national band distribution, the EPC-B and F/G crossing
  counts with their fuel composition, and the multiplier bootstrap intervals
  (Table 2 Panel A, Figure 3a).
- `fixed_factor_bootstrap_draws.csv` - all 1,000 within-fuel bootstrap replicates,
  including the three sampled multipliers and all four Table 2 Panel A quantities.
- `band_transition_observed_to_constant.csv` - Supplementary Table S8.
- `rounding_sensitivity.json` - threshold rounding sensitivity.
- `metered_vs_epc_validation.csv` - the DESNZ metered-energy comparison (Figure 2b).
- `multiplier_robustness.csv` - the reclassification re-estimated under more granular
  multiplier definitions, varied no-works tolerances, cross-fitting and the additive
  windfall alternative (Supplementary Table S6).
- `capex_equivalent.json` - affected floor area behind the crossings and its
  expenditure-equivalent scale.

`markov.run_markov` writes the reassessment-throughput sensitivity
(`markov_threshold_expected_state_results.csv` and the accompanying diagnostics,
back-test and structural-uncertainty tables; Supplementary Tables S12 to S18).

`robustness.run_robustness` writes the certificate-validity, England against Wales,
and stricter no-recorded-works checks (Supplementary Tables S9, S10 and S11).

`validation` writes the held-out bridge validation, the latest-frame
characteristics, the policy-proxy subsets and the ND-NEED representativeness
comparison.

`mae_rescue.main` writes the same-methodology reassessment noise floor used in
Supplementary Section 1.18 (`mae_rescue_same_method_noise.csv` and the accompanying
ladders and summary).

## Doubly robust estimates and their recomputation

The values reported in Table 2 Panel B, Figure 3 panels c and d, and Supplementary
Tables S29 to S32, S36 and S37 are held in `code/doubly_robust_estimates/` and are read
from there by `figures.py` and `paper_tables.py`. See
`code/doubly_robust_estimates/README.md`.

`dr_estimator.run_dr_estimator` reimplements the Stage-1 estimator from Supplementary
1.21 and writes:

- `dr_estimator_recomputed.csv` - plug-in term, residual correction, doubly robust and
  inverse-odds-weighted estimates for Models C and D at both thresholds.
- `dr_estimator_vs_reported.csv` - the recomputed values against the reported ones,
  with absolute and percentage differences.

## Threshold model selection and the expenditure scale

`bridge_analysis.run_bridge_analysis` writes the model-selection, calibration and
transport suite that underpins the threshold estimator, all prefixed `bridge_`:

- `bridge_area_model_nestedcv.csv`, `bridge_area_model_comparison.csv` - candidate
  affected-area models under nested cross-validation.
- `bridge_transport_diagnostics.csv`, `_estimates.csv`, `_oos_validation.csv`,
  `_smd.csv` - Stage-1 transport from the calibration sample to the register frame,
  with its common-support, balance and out-of-sample diagnostics.
- `bridge_calibration_*.csv`, `bridge_validation_*.csv` - out-of-fold calibration of
  the threshold-probability model by fuel, band and floor-area band.
- `bridge_sector_expenditure_*.csv`, `expenditure_equivalent_*.csv` - the
  sector-specific cost schedule and the expenditure-equivalent scale.
- `bridge_validation_report.md` - a human-readable summary of model selection,
  leakage checks, aggregate sensitivity estimates and interpretation limits.

## International typology

`typology.run_typology` writes `international_exposure_typology.csv` and `.tex`, the
per-country metric-sensitivity, grid-decline and exposure coding behind Figure 4 and
Supplementary Table S3. Grid-decline percentages come from the Ember series.

## France

`france.run_france` writes `france_summary.json` (the headline exit counts),
`france_by_heating.csv`, `france_transition_matrix.csv`, `france_window_robustness.csv`
and the quality-control, label-reproduction, binding-criterion, negative-control,
surface, duplicate and department tables that make up Supplementary Section 2.
`france_sanity_checks.json` records the internal consistency checks. A publication
run fails unless every check passes, including the exact 2,809,777-row source window,
all headline counts and percentages, and each Table 3 heating-group tuple.
